Acoustic Kit samples
====================

Source: "Big Rusty Drums" by Karoryfer Samples
https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.big-rusty-drums
License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication)
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Edits for DrumMashine: one velocity layer (round robin 1) per articulation,
close mic + overhead mixed to mono, leading silence trimmed, length capped,
cubic fade-out, peak-normalized to -1 dBFS, 44.1 kHz 16-bit WAV.
These edited files are also released under CC0 1.0.
